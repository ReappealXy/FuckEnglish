BrimDeck-derived island UI and themes

The compact geometry, hover bridge, panel layout, typography, settings palette
and bundled theme colors are adapted from MoFeng2223/BrimDeck.
Original project: https://github.com/MoFeng2223/BrimDeck
License: Apache License 2.0; see licenses/BrimDeck-APACHE-2.0.txt.
The Go/Preact adaptation replaces BrimDeck content with local Excel vocabulary.

Original theme JSON files and their authors, licenses and source links are
preserved in BrimDeck-themes/. Color extensions identify their respective
MIT-licensed upstream themes. Cyberpunk: Edgerunners is an unofficial color
palette; no artwork, logos or fonts from that series are included.
